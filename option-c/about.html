About us

Two Montessori schools where parents, teachers and children learn together.

Our story

A learning community for every family

We opened Amazing Beginnings Montessori in Inver Grove Heights in 2006 and Eagan Montessori Academy South in 2016. Our families come from many cultures and backgrounds, so children learn to work in diverse settings.

We believe children learn at their own pace, so we offer many ways to learn.

Since 2006

Our history

  1. 2006 Amazing Beginnings opens

    The first Montessori school in Inver Grove Heights.

  2. 2016 Eagan Montessori Academy South opens

    Our school on Rahncliff Court in Eagan.

  3. 2017 – 2024 Readers’ Choice Award

    Voted a best Montessori school eight years in a row.

  4. Today Two schools, one family

    NAEYC accredited, with a Parent Aware 4-star rating.
