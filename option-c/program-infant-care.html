Infant Childcare

Caring, Montessori-inspired care for babies from 6 weeks old.

A calm, safe start

Our infant rooms give babies a safe, interesting space to rest, explore and grow, with caring adults who watch and follow each child’s needs.

Full-time and part-time infant care are available at both schools.

Classrooms grouped by stage

  • Our tiniest classroom: from 6 weeks until babies start to crawl, with tummy time and lots of face-to-face play.
  • Crawling classroom: textured floors, toys and climbing equipment for busy movers.
  • Getting ready for toddlers: small tables and chairs, cot sleeping and eating on their own.

What makes our infant rooms special

  • 1:4 staff ratio

    In every infant classroom, with small groups by stage.

  • Daily journal

    Photos, notes and moments from your baby’s day.

  • Sign language & music

    Sign language from 6 months, music class from 12 months.

Infant care questions

Babies are grouped by stage: a room for the youngest (6 weeks to crawling), a crawling room, and a room that prepares for toddler class.

Infants follow their own individual sleep schedules.

You get a daily journal with photos and notes, plus a monthly newsletter.

Yes. Both full-time and part-time infant care are available.
