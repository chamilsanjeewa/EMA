Presenting tips

  • Use the A · B · C switcher at the bottom of every page to see the same page in another design.
  • Make the browser window narrow (or use the phone view in developer tools) to show the mobile menu.
  • Photos are free stock samples, not the school’s own photos. They will be replaced with real photos of the school.
  • The language switcher, forms and “Apply Now” buttons are demo only.
