Frequently asked questions

Quick answers about our schools, programs and enrollment.

About our schools

General questions

A child-centered approach where children learn by doing. They choose hands-on activities and work at their own pace in a carefully prepared classroom.

Children from 6 weeks to 8 years: infants, toddlers, preschool, Pre-K, private Kindergarten and Summer Bridge.

We are open 6:30am to 6pm, Monday to Friday, 12 months a year, except for announced holidays.

Eagan Montessori Academy South is at 1970 Rahncliff Court, Eagan. Amazing Beginnings Montessori is at 3105 65th St. East, Inver Grove Heights.

We are NAEYC accredited and have a four-star rating from Parent Aware, Minnesota’s top preschool rating.

Yes. Hot lunches are catered by CKC Good Food, and we serve snacks every day. You may also send your own lunch.

The main entrance has a coded entry system, and all staff are trained in CPR and First Aid.

Joining us

Enrollment and tuition

Call us or send a message. We will set up a tour and walk you through the next steps.

Call us with your start date. We then meet for a short intake interview and you fill out the enrollment form.

Tuition depends on age group and schedule. Full-time and part-time options are available. Contact us for details.

Some programs may have a waitlist. We encourage early applications and will keep you updated.

Yes, we give enrollment priority to siblings.
