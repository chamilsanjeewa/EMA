Five tips for a happy first day

Simple ways to help your child feel calm and ready for a new classroom.

Parent tips September 15, 2026 Both schools

Starting school is a big step for children and parents. These small habits can make the first weeks easier.

  1. Visit together first. A short visit helps the room feel familiar.
  2. Read about school. Picture books about school start good conversations.
  3. Keep goodbyes short. A hug, a smile and a clear “see you later” work best.
  4. Bring a comfort item. A family photo or small blanket can help.
  5. Keep a routine. Regular sleep and meal times help children settle in.

Our teachers are happy to share how your child’s day went at pick-up.
